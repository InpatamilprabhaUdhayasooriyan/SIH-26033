## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1.My idea is to develop a system that connects farmers directly with bulk buyers such as wholesalers, supermarkets, hotels, and food processing companies.
2.Farmers producing large quantities often cannot sell everything directly to consumers.
3.(Eg): A farmer growing tomatoes can sell the entire harvest to a bulk buyer instead of selling small quantities to individual consumers.
This reduces intermediaries and helps farmers get better prices

## Technical Approach
1.I propose a simple digital platform with local farmer assistance centres.
2.Farmers can register their products with the help of centre staff or through a phone call.
3.The system connects them with bulk buyers by comparing quantity, price, and location.
4.Eg: If five farmers grow tomatoes in the same village, their produce can be combined and sold to a bulk buyer.





## Feasibility and Viability
.The system can use existing technologies such as databases and mobile applications.
2.Farmers who cannot use smartphones can get help from local assistance centres.
3.The system can be maintained through a small service fee.

## Impact and Benefits
1.Farmer earns more,consumer pays less,wastage reduced.
2.Helps farmers find more buyers and get better prices.
3.consumers get products at reasonable prices.
4.Reduces marketing and transportation costs.
5.group selling also reduces.
6.(Eg):unsold tomatoes can be sold to bulk buyers before they spoil.
